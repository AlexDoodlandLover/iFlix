About iFlix

Welcome to iFlix! It's a retro-style video platform where you can:

  • Watch videos uploaded by other users (skate fails, funny cats, or anything 2010-ish).
  • Upload your own videos from your device gallery or files.
  • Use an anonymous username if you like privacy.
  • Subscribe to other users and get notifications when they post.
  • Simulate iOS 6 or classic devices to feel like it’s really old-school.

How it works:

  1. Pick a tab (Home, Subs, Upload, About).
  2. Upload or watch videos.
  3. If you subscribe to someone, iFlix will tell you when they post something new.
  4. Retro vibes only! Keep the titles like “My 6 Skating Fails” or “Funny Cats for 2010”.

Enjoy the nostalgia!
