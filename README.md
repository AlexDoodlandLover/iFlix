# 🎬 iFlix – Retro 2010-Style Video App 📺

[![Version](https://img.shields.io/badge/version-1.0.0-blue)](https://github.com/)
[![Platform](https://img.shields.io/badge/platform-iOS%204--6-lightgrey)](https://github.com/)

Welcome to **iFlix**, the ultimate throwback HTML5 web app!  
It’s like opening a tiny iPod Touch in 2010 and discovering **Funny Cats**, **Skate Fails**, and other pixel-perfect, old-school videos.  

> Relive the early days of iOS video apps… with full retro vibes! 😎✨

---

## 🚀 Features

- **Anonymous usernames** – no sign-up, no email, pure anonymity 🕶️  
- **Upload videos** – must be classic 2010 content: cats, skate fails, etc. 🐱🛹  
- **Mandatory retro titles** – e.g., *"My 6 Skating Fails"*, *"Funny Cats for 2010"* 📝  
- **Subscriptions** – track your favorite anonymous creators ❤️  
- **Fake push notifications** – iOS-style alerts for new uploads 🔔  
- **Skeuomorphic UI** – glossy buttons, tab bars, and retro fonts ✨  
- **Standalone mode support** – add to your home screen, no Safari chrome 🏠  
- **Old iOS compatibility** – tested on Safari iOS 4–6 📱  

---

## 🎯 Installation / Usage

1. Open `index.html` in **Safari** on your iOS device.  
2. Tap the **Share** button → **Add to Home Screen**.  
3. Launch **iFlix** from your home screen – fully standalone, like a real app.  
4. Upload videos, browse the feed, subscribe to users, and enjoy retro notifications!  

> 💡 Tip: Videos are hosted externally, so provide a direct MP4 link.

---

## 📌 Upload Rules

- Only upload **2010-worthy content**:  
  - Funny cats doing weird things 🐾  
  - Skateboarding fails & epic tricks 🛹  
  - Other retro-style, low-res, shaky videos 🎥  
- Title must include keywords like `skate`, `fail`, `cat`, or `2010`  
- If the upload doesn’t match the vibe, the app politely rejects it 😏
